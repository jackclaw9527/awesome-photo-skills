Retratos de mascotas, composiciones familiares, recuerdos de parejas e imágenes de hitos. Estas son plantillas públicas no probadas y de nueva creación. Personalice los marcadores de posición entre corchetes con datos y materiales proporcionados por el usuario; no los describa como las indicaciones que produjeron ejemplos de repositorio.

## Retrato de mascota personalizado (I026)

Convierte una foto de mascota en un retrato listo para exhibir.

- **Entradas:** Fotos de mascotas: obligatorias
- **Preservar:** Mantenga la identidad, las marcas, el color de ojos, la forma de las orejas y las proporciones del cuerpo de la mascota.
- **Cambiar:** Cambia la ambientación, iluminación, paleta y tratamiento artístico.
- **Verificar:** Compara ojos, orejas, marcas y silueta antes de juzgar el estilo.
- **Detalles de la tarea:** [dearsketch](https://dearsketch.com/es/intent/i026-turn-a-pet-photo-into-a-custom-portrait.html?utm_source=github&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=dearsketch-public-cases&utm_content=i026_prompt_i026)

```text
Create one custom pet portrait from [PET PHOTO].
The subject is [PET NAME OR “NO NAME”] and the intended style is [ART STYLE].
Preserve the exact animal identity, facial structure, eye color, coat pattern, ear shape, and body proportions.
Do not invent collars, tags, scars, or markings that are not visible or specified.
Replace the original surroundings with [BACKGROUND OR SETTING].
Use [COLOR PALETTE] and [LIGHTING MOOD] while keeping the pet clearly recognizable.
Compose the animal as [HEADSHOT / HALF BODY / FULL BODY] for [DISPLAY USE].
Keep anatomy natural, with clean paws, whiskers, fur edges, and believable depth.
Do not add text unless [EXACT TEXT] is supplied.
Deliver one finished portrait image; do not create a sheet, sequence, or alternate version.
```

## Retrato de mascota real (I028)

Coloque una mascota real en un concepto original de retrato real o histórico.

- **Entradas:** Fotos de mascotas: obligatorias
- **Conservar:** Conserva la cara, el patrón del pelaje, el color de ojos y la anatomía específica de la especie de la mascota.
- **Cambio:** Cambia de ropa, accesorios, fondo y atmósfera inspirada en la época.
- **Verificar:** Confirma que el disfraz nunca reemplaza a la mascota con un animal de apariencia diferente.
- **Detalles de la tarea:** [dearsketch](https://dearsketch.com/es/intent/i028-turn-a-pet-into-a-royal-or-historical-portrait.html?utm_source=github&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=dearsketch-public-cases&utm_content=i028_prompt_i028)

```text
Create one playful royal portrait using [PET PHOTO] as the identity reference.
Dress the pet in an original [ROYAL / HISTORICAL-INSPIRED] costume for [THEME].
Preserve the exact face, eyes, muzzle, ears, coat colors, markings, and body proportions.
Keep the animal’s species anatomy believable beneath the costume.
Design clothing that fits naturally without hiding the defining face or coat pattern.
Use an original setting with [BACKGROUND], [PALETTE], and [LIGHTING].
Avoid copying a named artwork, living artist, or protected character.
Do not imply that the scene is a real historical record.
Add no wording unless [EXACT CAPTION] is supplied; reproduce supplied wording exactly.
Deliver one finished portrait image only.
```

## Retrato familiar con mascotas (I021)

Combine las referencias de familiares y mascotas proporcionadas en un retrato coherente.

- **Entradas:** Fotos de miembros de la familia: obligatorias; Fotos de mascotas: obligatorias
- **Preservar:** Mantenga a cada persona y mascota individualmente reconocibles, con el recuento y las marcas correctos.
- **Cambio:** Cambia el entorno compartido, la coordinación del vestuario, la luz y la disposición.
- **Verificar:** Cuente cada tema y verifique si hay omisiones, duplicados o intercambios de identidad.
- **Detalles de la tarea:** [dearsketch](https://dearsketch.com/es/intent/i021-create-a-family-portrait-with-pets.html?utm_source=github&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=dearsketch-public-cases&utm_content=i021_prompt_i021)

```text
Create one family portrait from [LABELED FAMILY MEMBER PHOTOS] and [LABELED PET PHOTOS].
Include exactly [NUMBER OF PEOPLE] people and [NUMBER OF PETS] pets in [REQUESTED ARRANGEMENT].
Preserve each person’s identity, age cues, skin tone, hair, and visible defining features.
Preserve each pet’s species, face, coat colors, markings, size, and body type.
Do not merge faces, duplicate subjects, swap markings, or invent an absent family member.
Coordinate [WARDROBE DIRECTION] without making everyone identical.
Place the group in [SETTING] with consistent perspective, scale, shadows, and eye lines.
Keep hands, paws, leashes, and body overlaps natural and readable.
Use no text unless [EXACT TEXT] is supplied.
Deliver one complete group portrait image only.
```

Create one baby milestone portrait from [BABY PHOTO].
Use only the parent-confirmed milestone “[EXACT AGE OR MILESTONE]” and optional date “[CONFIRMED DATE]”.
Preserve the baby’s identity, skin tone, facial features, hair, and age-appropriate proportions.
Keep pose, hands, feet, and support surfaces physically safe and believable.
Design [BACKDROP THEME] with [PALETTE] and a small number of [PROPS].
Do not infer age, birth date, weight, name, or developmental achievement from the photo.
