## Selection rules

- Prefer the narrowest task whose inputs and delivery boundary match the request.
- If the user provides a real person, pet, product, property, interface, brand asset, or design, retain its identity and factual details.
- When a task depends on exact text or data, keep the supplied facts verbatim and require proofreading.
- When the output is only a concept or preview, say so before presenting the prompt.
