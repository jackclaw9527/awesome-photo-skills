# Product Photo Editing & Ecommerce Prompts · 商品修图与电商展示

[All 50 prompts](../prompts.md) · [中文首页](../../README.md) · [English](../../README.en.md)

Keep the same product while changing how it is presented: a clean listing image, a lifestyle scene, an on-model clothing preview or a product information layout. Product shape, color and label accuracy matter more than decorative effects.

先核对商品结构、配色与标签，再看光线和场景。尺寸、卖点与规格使用真实资料；透明背景和印刷交付必须检查文件本身。

## Choose the right task

Provide a sharp product photo. Add dimensions, approved claims and exact copy for information layouts. Provide the actual garment for an on-model image; an attractive render does not prove fit.
