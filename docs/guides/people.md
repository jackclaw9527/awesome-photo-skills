# Portrait & Personal Photo Editing Prompts · 人像编辑与个人形象

[中文](../../README.md) · [English](../../README.en.md) · [Português](../../README.pt.md) · [Español](../../README.es.md) · [Français](../../README.fr.md) · [Deutsch](../../README.de.md) · [عربي](../../README.ar.md) · [русский](../../README.ru.md) · [日本語](../../README.ja.md) · [हिंदी](../../README.hi.md)


Choose between a professional headshot, a targeted clothing edit, a virtual try-on or an illustrated identity. A headshot should retain natural facial features; a cartoon can change the medium while keeping the person recognizable.

先决定要改变衣服、配饰、背景还是整张图的表现形式。试戴指定眼镜和纹身图案时，需要提供对应原稿，不能只给一个模糊的风格词。

## Choose the right task

For a local edit, name the exact area to change and what must stay untouched. For a specific accessory or tattoo, attach its reference as well as the person photo.

| Task | Preserve | Change | Prompt & inputs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Professional Headshot from a Selfie / 自拍转职业头像 | Keep identity, facial geometry, skin tone, age cues, and natural texture. | Change crop, background, lighting, and professional wardrobe styling. | [I001](../cases/i001-create-a-professional-linkedin-headshot-from-a-selfie.md#public-prompt) |
| Replace Casual Clothing with Professional Attire / 休闲装换正装 | Keep identity, face, hair, pose, body shape, hands, and background unless requested. | Change garment type, color, fit, and visible accessories. | [I015](../cases/i015-replace-casual-clothing-with-professional-attire.md#public-prompt) |
| Historical-Inspired Personal Portrait / 皇家与复古人物肖像 | Keep the person’s identity, age cues, skin tone, and facial structure. | Change costume, background, props, light, and period-inspired atmosphere. | [I052](../cases/i052-turn-yourself-into-a-royal-vintage-or-historical-portrait.md#public-prompt) |
| Virtual Eyewear Preview / 眼镜试戴预览 | Keep identity, face shape, skin, eyes, and the supplied frame details for the chosen branch. | Change only frame placement, lens appearance, and minimal lighting integration. | [I036](../cases/i036-virtually-try-on-glasses.md#public-prompt) |
| Tattoo Placement Preview / 纹身上身预览 | Keep the person, skin texture, body anatomy, and tattoo artwork unchanged. | Change only placement, scale, curvature, opacity, and integration. | [I038](../cases/i038-preview-a-tattoo-on-a-body-photo.md#public-prompt) |
| Cartoon Avatar from a Portrait / 照片转卡通头像 | Keep face shape, hair, skin tone, and visible signature accessories. | Change rendering language, simplification, background, and palette. | [I047](../cases/i047-turn-a-portrait-photo-into-a-cartoon-avatar.md#public-prompt) |
| Anime-Inspired Avatar / 照片转动漫角色 | Keep face shape, skin tone, hair, eye relationship, and defining accessories. | Change line work, shading, palette, background, and stylization level. | [I048](../cases/i048-turn-a-portrait-photo-into-an-anime-character.md#public-prompt) |
| Packaged Action Figure Concept / 包装行动人偶 | Keep the person’s identity and requested accessory list. | Change body into a toy-like figure, packaging, color system, and display layout. | [I050](../cases/i050-turn-a-person-into-a-packaged-action-figure.md#public-prompt) |
| Single Chat Sticker / 照片转聊天贴纸 | Keep the subject recognizable and retain defining markings or features. | Change expression, pose emphasis, outline, background removal, and sticker styling. | [I056](../cases/i056-turn-a-photo-into-chat-stickers.md#public-prompt) |
| Personalized Movie Poster / 个人与情侣电影海报 | Keep every person’s identity and all confirmed billing text. | Change cinematic setting, genre treatment, lighting, composition, and typography concept. | [I252](../cases/i252-turn-a-personal-or-couple-photo-into-a-movie-poster.md#public-prompt) |
