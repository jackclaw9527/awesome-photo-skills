Choose between a professional headshot, a targeted clothing edit, a virtual try-on or an illustrated identity. A headshot should retain natural facial features; a cartoon can change the medium while keeping the person recognizable.

Decide first whether you want to change the clothing, accessories, background, or the presentation of the entire image. When trying on designated glasses and tattoo designs, you need to provide the corresponding originals, not just a vague style word.

## Choose the right task

For a local edit, name the exact area to change and what must stay untouched. For a specific accessory or tattoo, attach its reference as well as the person photo.
