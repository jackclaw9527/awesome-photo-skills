Turn pet and family photographs into portraits and keepsakes while keeping every subject recognizable. Choose a single-subject portrait, a couple artwork or a composite that brings separate photographs together.

For pet portraits, the eyes, ear shapes and patterns should be preserved first; for multi-person synthesis, all members, relationships and arrangements should be listed first. The commemorative date and inscription are provided by you, do not guess from the photos.

## Choose the right task

One pet photo is enough for a pet portrait. A family composite needs every person or pet you want included. For a keepsake with names or dates, provide the exact wording separately.
