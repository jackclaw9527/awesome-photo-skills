# 5 basic templates you can try directly

[中文](../../README.md) · [English](../../README.en.md) · [Português](../../README.pt.md) · [Español](../../README.es.md) · [Français](../../README.fr.md) · [Deutsch](../../README.de.md) · [عربي](../../README.ar.md) · [русский](../../README.ru.md) · [日本語](../../README.ja.md) · [हिंदी](../../README.hi.md)


Here are 5 base templates written individually for public content libraries. After uploading your own materials, give the corresponding text to a tool that supports reference image editing and you can try it. Different tools have different input methods, costs and available capabilities. Please refer to the description of the tool used.

These templates were not exported from the website's private skills, nor were they used to generate the existing sample diagrams in this repository. They have not been tested on a template-by-template model and are not guaranteed to reproduce the example diagrams; the example diagrams are marked separately from the tutorial templates.

## 1 pet custom portrait

Prepare a photo of your pet that shows the eyes, ears, and main pattern. The following example chooses a warm white background and simple illustrations as the creative direction, which can be replaced by yourself.

```text
请以所附照片中的同一只宠物为主体，制作一张简洁的宠物肖像插画。
保留照片里可见的脸部结构、眼睛颜色、耳朵形状和主要花纹，
不要把它替换成另一只相似品种的宠物。
使用暖白背景、柔和色块和少量线条，让主体清楚、构图留有呼吸空间。
不添加姓名、日期、服饰、配件或水印。只输出一张最终肖像。
```

Check that the patterns match, that the ears are intact, and that you still recognize the same pet.

## 2 Convert real photos to cartoon avatars

Prepare a clear portrait. Featuring flat blocks of color, this basic template doesn't require the reproduction of any specific work or artist's expression.

```text
把所附照片中的人物转换成原创的卡通头像。
保留可识别的脸型、发型和照片里确实存在的配饰。
用清楚的轮廓、简洁色块和克制的阴影表达人物，背景保持单纯。
不要改变人物年龄观感，不加入照片中不存在的职业、身份或品牌元素。
头部和肩部构图，不加文字和水印，只输出一张头像。
```

Check that the character is recognisable, and that hair edges, glasses and ears look natural.

## 3 Residential watercolor commemorative paintings

Prepare a photo of the house that shows the main exterior facade. Only draw building information that can be confirmed by photos.

```text
根据所附房屋照片制作一张水彩风格的住宅纪念画。
保留房屋的主要轮廓、屋顶、门窗位置和可见结构。
用轻薄的水彩色层和少量线条表达建筑，周围保留适量空白。
不增加照片看不到的新楼层、门牌、文字或虚构建筑装饰。
背景可以适度简化，但不要把原房屋改成另一栋建筑。只输出一张画。
```

Check the watercolor representation first against the roof, windows and entrance locations.

## 4 Product main image with white background

Prepare a clear photo of the product; transparent, reflective and small hollow structures require special inspection.

```text
把所附照片中的同一件商品整理成干净的白底商品展示图。
保持商品本身的形状、比例、颜色、标签和可见细节，不更换商品型号。
移除与商品无关的背景杂物，让商品完整入画，边缘自然。
不要添加新的标签、卖点、品牌、配件或认证标记。
不添加装饰边框和文字，只输出一张最终商品图。
```

Check the small edges and labels, and then check the size, white space and main image specifications according to the actual sales channel.

## 5 Logo Concept Exploration

Replace the content in the brackets with your own branding material. Graphic concepts are requested here, with no commitment to vector files or trademark registrability.

```text
为一个[品牌领域]品牌探索一个原创图形标志概念。
品牌定位是[一句话定位]，主要面向[目标人群]。
用简洁形状表达[一个核心意象]，选择[主色]搭配中性色。
先只展示一个不含文字的图形标志，居中放在纯净背景上。
不要套用现有品牌标志，不加样机、装饰场景、口号或水印。
只输出一张概念图。
```

Zoom out to see if it is still clear, and then do the word mark, vector arrangement and usage scenario verification.

## From template to own results

First complete a small-scale attempt to check whether the subject and facts are correct, and then change the painting style, composition or other variables. When encountering name, price, size, event date and accurate copywriting, you should check them word for word and do not let the model fill them in for you.

If you want to view the input requirements by specific tasks, you can enter the corresponding page of the website from the [50 case directory ](./prompts.md).
