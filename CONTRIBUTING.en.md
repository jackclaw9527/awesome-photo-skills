Welcome to add cases that clearly explain "what you put in, what you do, and what you get". Prioritize submission of tasks with clear visual distinctions, easy input preparation, and clear limitations.

A case please include:

1. Short name, applicable people and usage scenarios.
2. Required and optional materials, accurate copy and sources of data.
3. Input examples, result examples and their respective sources and licenses.
4. Production steps and checkpoints.
5. Known limitations and which parts require additional tooling.

If reusable prompt words are provided, please indicate which words are actually used and which are later compiled tutorials to avoid confusion. Do not call examples guaranteed results or unvalidated model comparisons.

Do not submit user profiles, non-redacted orders, access tokens, full supplier responses, or temporary signed links in Issues or Pull Requests.
